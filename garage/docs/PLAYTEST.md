# Parent playtest sheet: Wonder Garage

Bentley hasn't played the game yet. These three short sessions are how we find out whether it works for him.
The answers decide what changes next. Please don't coach him. Just watch.
If he wants to stop, stopping is a good outcome too.

**Before session 1:** open the game once while online so it can save itself for offline play (grown-up settings →
Offline should say *Ready to play without internet*). Use the iPad in landscape. Help settings stay at their defaults
unless you've already seen that he needs *More help* or *Slower driving*.

Write what you saw, not what you hoped for. "Tapped Play right away" is more useful than "loved it".

### What to look for (and what not to read into it)

- **Playing longer doesn't mean the game is better.** A short, happy session that ends at a mission boundary is a
  good result.
- The game doesn't promise behaviour change. The friendship moments (taking turns, picking a route sign) are optional
  pretend play. They aren't lessons or tests.
- Asking for help, watching quietly, or skipping the invite-a-friend button are all normal ways to play.
- If he repeatedly misses a jump, check whether the helper example appeared after two tries. If he needed you every
  time, try **More help** in the grown-up settings next session and write down that you changed it.
- Note how he holds the iPad and which fingers press the gas, brake and jump, and whether he backs up on purpose. If
  driving itself gets in the way of the story, the grown-up setting **Driving → drives itself** is the earlier,
  simpler version (only jump and brake). Write down which one he played.
- On the Test Track, note what he goes back to: crates, flips, the high deck, the mud. That says what to put in the
  next missions.
